The Challenge

Why Satellite Data Matters

Millions of people in Punjab's villages lack access to timely, accurate information about their land, crops, and environment. Traditional tools are expensive, complex, or simply unavailable.

🌾

Crop Monitoring

Farmers need to know when crops are stressed, but ground surveys are slow and costly. Satellite NDVI gives instant vegetation health.

💧

Water Management

Tracking water bodies, irrigation, and drought conditions from space helps communities plan and respond faster.

🏘️

Land Use

Understanding how land is used — agriculture, urban, forest — is critical for planning, but hard to map without tools.

🔥

Climate Impact

From heat waves to flooding, satellite data helps communities prepare for and respond to climate-related disasters.

Limits of Our Conclusions

Satellite data is powerful, but it has important limitations. Here's what we can and cannot conclude from this analysis:

☁️

Cloud Cover

Clouds block optical satellite imagery, creating gaps in data. Some areas may have incomplete coverage, especially during monsoon season.

📐

Spatial Resolution

At 10 m per pixel, small features like narrow roads, small ponds, or individual trees cannot be detected. Mixed pixels at boundaries blur the true extent of land cover types.

🔄

Seasonal Variation

NDVI naturally fluctuates with seasons — crops grow, harvest, and fields lie fallow. A single snapshot cannot distinguish long-term trends from normal seasonal cycles.

❓

What vs. Why

Satellites show what changed, but not why. A drop in NDVI could mean drought, crop disease, land conversion, or sensor error — ground investigation is needed to determine the cause.

📅

Sensor & Date Comparability

Different satellites, sensors, or acquisition dates can produce varying results due to atmospheric conditions, sun angle, and sensor calibration differences.

🌍

Ground Verification

All satellite-derived conclusions require field validation. Without ground truth data, our maps and statistics remain estimates with inherent uncertainty.
